# AI Me V1: Learning Prototype

**Responsible Technology. Stronger Governance. Safeguarding. Children's Rights.**

AI Me is a learning prototype developed by **Little Voices Advisory**, exploring how technology can help organisations identify governance gaps when using AI-enabled filming in children's and youth sport.

## Try AI Me

**[Launch AI Me V1](https://aimeeongley.github.io/ai-me-v1/)**

## What does AI Me do?

AI Me follows three steps:

- **KNOW:** Explore selected evidence-backed findings about a technology.
- **CHECK:** Answer six questions about your organisation's practices.
- **ACT:** Identify potential governance gaps and practical actions, with a printable report.

## Current scope and limitations

AI Me V1 is an early learning prototype, not a finished commercial product.

- It currently examines only **Veo**, using three selected evidence-backed findings.
- It is not a comprehensive review of Veo or a comparison of technology providers.
- Assessment results are based on information supplied by the user and are not independently verified.
- Vendor policies and practices may change.

AI Me does not provide legal advice, certify compliance or guarantee that a technology or practice is safe. Organisations remain responsible for their own governance, privacy and safeguarding decisions.

## Why I built it

My research into AI-enabled filming in children's sport raised questions about consent, privacy, safeguarding and accountability.

I wanted to explore whether I could translate some of that research into a practical digital tool.

With no previous coding experience, I began learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, JSON and GitHub.

AI Me V1 is the result of that learning journey. It demonstrates how research and governance principles can begin to be translated into an interactive tool.
