AI Me V1 is a learning prototype developed by Little Voices Advisory.

This assessment provides general information to help organisations identify potential governance gaps and practical next steps when using AI-enabled filming in children's sport.

About this assessment

This assessment is about your organisation's use of the selected technology involving participants under 18. Please answer based on your under-18 activities, even if your organisation uses the technology differently with adults.

The same technology can create different governance issues depending on which features are enabled and how they are actually used.

1. Which parts of this technology does your organisation use for activities involving participants under 18?

Select everything you know is currently used. If you are unsure, that is okay. AI Me will treat uncertainty as something to establish, not as a yes or no.

2. Who may be recorded or analysed when your organisation uses this technology?

Select everyone who may be captured, even if they do not have an account or use the technology themselves.

3. What happens if a child or parent does not agree to the child being recorded?

Select the option that best describes what currently happens in your organisation.

3A. Which controls does your organisation currently use?

Select everything that currently applies. Do not select a control unless you know it is being used.

3B. When these controls are used, is the child still captured in the original recording?

We are asking about the original footage before any blurring, access restrictions or deletion takes place.

4. Does your organisation keep a current record of each child's filming and data permissions?

This means a record that lets your organisation identify what each child or parent has agreed to, declined or later changed.

5. Does your organisation keep a record of deletion requests and the actions taken?

This includes recording who the request relates to, what was requested, what action was taken and any evidence confirming deletion.

6. If a child or parent asks for their footage or data to be deleted, can your organisation identify and locate the relevant footage or data?

Think about recordings, clips, downloads, shared copies and other data that may relate to the child.
